Mathematics for Disaster Preparedness

Topic: Population Distribution and Emergency Supply Computation

1. Population per Evacuation Center Suppose a community has 600 residents and 3 evacuation centers.

Formula:

Population per center = Total Population ÷ Number of Evacuation Centers

600 ÷ 3 = 200 people

Therefore, each evacuation center should be prepared to accommodate approximately 200 people.

2. Emergency Water Supply

If each person needs 3 liters of water per day:

Given:

N=200 people & R=3litres/per person

Unknown=V

Total Volume of Water=Number of People×Requirement per Person

Sol'n:200 × 3 = 600 liters

Each evacuation center should prepare at least 600 liters of water per day.

3. Simple Mathematical Model

Water needed = Number of people × 3 liters

People / Water Needed

100 /300 L

200 /600 L

300 /900 L

400 /1,200 L

500 /1,500 L

Explanation & Pattern:

-this is a linear model: amount of water increases by a constant of 300 liters for every additional 100 people.

-pattern: as population rises,required water rises proportionally.

Conclusion: This model let us quickly and accurately calculate water supply requirments for any population size in evacuation centers.
